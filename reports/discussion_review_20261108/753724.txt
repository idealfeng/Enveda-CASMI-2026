Since mass spectra identification in CASMI 2026 needs a solid candidate database for unknown spectra, I spent time cleaning, desalting, and organizing a reference database of ~1.8 Crore (18M+) chemical structures so you don't have to spend hours processing raw files from scratch:


📦 Dataset: CASMI 2026 Candidate Database


coconut/: 460k natural products from COCONUT 2.0.

pubchem/: Bio-active small molecules (100–1200 Da, 8–75 heavy atoms).

100% deduplicated on 14-character InChIKey scaffolds.


🚀 Starter Notebook: 20M+ Candidate Retrieval Baseline


Streams all shards in < 250MB RAM using Polars.

Inverts all 10 competition adduct mass offsets.

Runs binary search to fetch matching structures in < 2 milliseconds.

Draws 2D molecular structures with RDKit.



Hope this saves you time and GPU memory for your training and inference pipelines.
