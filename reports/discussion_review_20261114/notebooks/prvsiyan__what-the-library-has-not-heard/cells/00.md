# What the Library Has Not Heard

A replay of [Analog Propagation](https://www.kaggle.com/code/prvsiyan/analog-propagation-casmi-2026-baseline) version 47 (public score 0.487) on molecules that the CASMI 2026 training file does not contain.

Why: version 48 of that notebook added a fragment re-ranker that had gained +0.012 to +0.014 MRR@25 on our held-out pools, and it then scored 0.486 against 0.487. Those pools were ordered by the neural scorers alone and lacked the pipeline's own evidence; once version 47's fragment score was added to them, the gain shrank to +0.001 to +0.004. A change has to be judged on the lists the whole pipeline writes. This notebook runs the whole pipeline on molecules none of its models has seen, and scores every layer it writes.

**This version is the timing run:** the whole pipeline on the first 30 of the 190 query molecules (110 spectra), on a T4, to measure how long a larger run would take. Its scores are in `replay_results/layer_scores.csv`; with 30 molecules they are too noisy to judge any change.
