<div style="font-size:11px;font-weight:850;letter-spacing:1.2px;color:#68E6CC;margin-bottom:10px;">THE V29 BREAKTHROUGH</div>
<div style="font-size:14px;color:#E4F8F3;line-height:1.8;">Reproducing <a href="https://www.kaggle.com/code/analyticaobscura/casmi-2026" style="color:#68E6CC;font-weight:800;text-decoration:underline;">OzanM's molecular-network ranking approach</a> brought a new kind of evidence into our pipeline: relationships between experimental spectra of different test molecules. Together with mass-based ranking and external reference matching, the reproduced system reached <strong style="color:#68E6CC;">Public MRR@25 = 0.488</strong>, surpassing our previous 0.487 baseline.</div>
<div style="font-size:13px;color:#A8D8D3;line-height:1.75;margin-top:11px;">Two follow-up experiments tested whether the new system would benefit from additional isomer reranking or a more permissive external-library match. Neither surpassed V29. It remains our strongest observed configuration.</div>
</div>
<div style="font-size:13px;color:#B6CDDE;line-height:1.75;margin-top:17px;">An earlier lesson still matters: V24 preserved the original 0.487 score with a more conservative reranker, but did not improve the baseline. V29 was our first displayed score increase beyond it.</div>
