| Question to investigate | Where to look — and why |
|:--|:--|
| What might a specific fragment represent? | **MassBank** — experimental MS/MS and acquisition conditions |
| Has a similar spectrum been recorded before? | **GNPS MASST** — search similar spectra across public collections |
| Does the instrument affect the result? | **MoNA** — spectra and acquisition metadata |
| One mass — how many structures? | **PubChem** — molecular formulas, isomers and chemical structures |
| How can molecular fragmentation be formalized? | **SIRIUS / CSI:FingerID** — fragment formulas, fragmentation trees and structure search |
