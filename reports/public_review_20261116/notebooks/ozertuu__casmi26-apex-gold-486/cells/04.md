**2.54 million rows are not 2.54 million independent molecules.** The same structure may be measured dozens of times at different collision energies, with different adducts, instruments and libraries.

In a spectrum, `m/z` gives peak position and intensity represents its relative strength. `inchikey14` encodes molecular connectivity; `ingest_lib` identifies the spectral source, **not** the instrument type.

**Our trail:** how many unique structures are present → which structures dominate the measurements → why does an exact molecular formula still fail to identify a unique molecule?
