## 36 · The Lineup: Can Simple Cosine Retrieve a Known Structure?

We have collected the clues. Now comes a small trial **without a trained neural model**.

For each eligible structure, choose two measured spectra: one query and one known reference. Add **different structures with nearby precursor m/z**, the same adduct and instrument, and preferably the same library. Rank the references using symmetric cosine similarity.

**This is a controlled diagnostic retrieval exercise, not the competition metric.** The correct structure is included by design, the cohort is enriched, and repeated measurements of known molecules are allowed. The experiment is nevertheless useful: it reveals where the known structure ranks and where elementary spectral matching fails.

The next stage of the investigation would move from this small exercise to a realistic candidate universe, retrieval recall, grouped validation, and carefully attributed public ranking methods.
