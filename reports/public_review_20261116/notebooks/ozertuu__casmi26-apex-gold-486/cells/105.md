## 37 · How Many Observations Permit a Fair Comparison?

The full collection is enormous, but stronger controls impose stronger requirements. Matching a molecular structure alone is not enough; we also need documented MS/MS acquisition conditions. This is **not a random subset of train**. We deliberately select structures with sufficient repeat measurements.

The scale of each experiment matters: **2.54 million** spectra describe the full train; **10,299** spectra form the targeted subset; **221** structures admit strict near-energy versus far-energy comparisons; **274** queries form two-candidate isomer lineups. These are different experimental populations, not successive steps in a single attrition funnel.
