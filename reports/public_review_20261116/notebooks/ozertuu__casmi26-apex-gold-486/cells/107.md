## 38 · Does Higher Collision Energy Create New Peaks or Merely Reweight Existing Ones?

**One molecular graph, two different readings.** We hold the structure, `ingest_lib`, adduct and instrument constant. For each structure we identify one near-energy pair (**difference greater than 0 but at most 5 eV**) and one far-energy pair (difference at least 20 eV). For the higher-energy spectrum in each pair, we measure the fraction of its **total intensity** falling on peaks unmatched to the lower-energy spectrum within ±0.02 Da.

Each point represents one structure: **221 structures** have both types of comparison. This prevents heavily remeasured molecules from dominating the summary.
