## 39 · Two Isomers, One Formula: Which One Will Cosine Choose?

This time the **candidate space genuinely shares the same molecular formula**. Each mini-task contains two distinct `inchikey14` structures observed in the same library, with the same recorded adduct and instrument type. The query and the correct reference are **different measured spectra**, no more than 5 eV apart. Each competitor receives one reference with the nearest available collision energy.

We found **274 queries across 164 molecular formulas**. The candidate space is deliberately tiny — exactly two choices per query. High accuracy here does not imply success among hundreds or thousands of chemical structures.
