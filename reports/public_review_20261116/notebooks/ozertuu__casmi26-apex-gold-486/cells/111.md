## 40 · Why Can Square-Root Intensity Weighting Outperform Elaborate Heuristics?

When one peak dominates the intensity, ordinary cosine can effectively become a comparison of a single ion. Taking square roots reduces the influence of dominant peaks without making every peak equally important.

We compare **the same 274 queries**, with the same two candidates and the same mass tolerance. Only the intensity weighting changes. One control retains just the 20 most intense peaks; another increases the weight of rare m/z bins using IDF computed from **1,572 structures whose formulas are absent from the 164 test formulas**.
