## 42 · One InChIKey14 Across Libraries: Transferability or Matching Protocols?

The training set contains repeated structures from different sources. For cross-library pairs we require **the same InChIKey14, the same adduct and Δcollision energy ≤ 5 eV**. We compare pairs with the same and different recorded instrument types.

The two matched groups show distinct cosine distributions. However, **this is not a clean causal estimate of instrument effects**: the libraries differ in compound coverage, recording protocols and preprocessing. The observation unit here is a spectrum pair, not necessarily an independent molecule.
