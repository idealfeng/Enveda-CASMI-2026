## 43 · Is a Rare Peak Always a More Informative Clue?

The intuition is appealing: a fragment rarely seen in other molecules might deserve a larger weight. Rather than assume this, we perform a **negative-control experiment**.

For each `0.02 Da` m/z bin, we estimate IDF using spectra from **1,572 structures** whose molecular formulas are outside the 164 formulas used in the isomer lineups. Each `inchikey14` contributes at most one vote per bin. We apply these weights to the same two candidates across the same 274 duels, keeping mass tolerance and query selection fixed.
