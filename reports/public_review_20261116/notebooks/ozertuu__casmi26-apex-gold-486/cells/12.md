## 03 · Who Owns 43% of the Spectra?

There are roughly nine spectra per structure on average. But that average hides a strong imbalance. Measure how much of **the full training set** comes from the most frequently observed 0.1%, 1%, 5%, and 10% of structures.

**Why it matters:** a randomly selected training row is much more likely to represent a frequently measured molecule than a rare one. This matters when defining training weights and validation protocols.
