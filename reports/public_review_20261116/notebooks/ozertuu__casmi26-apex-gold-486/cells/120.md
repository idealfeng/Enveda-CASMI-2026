## 44 · Eighteen Wrong Answers — Eighteen Different Chemical Problems?

**At first glance:** the correct structure wins 256 of 274 isomer duels. But multiple duels may involve **the same molecular formula**. Related difficulties then occur repeatedly, and counting every duel separately gives those formula groups extra weight.

**Control.** Group all 274 comparisons by formula and count how many distinct formula groups contain errors. Compare accuracy when each duel carries equal weight with accuracy when each formula carries equal weight. To estimate uncertainty, bootstrap **whole formula groups**, not individual duels.

**Why it matters:** when developing a candidate ranker, we need to know how many distinct families of errors we actually fix, rather than merely how many comparison rows become correct.
