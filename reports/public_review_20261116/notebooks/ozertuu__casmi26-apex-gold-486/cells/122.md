## 45 · Does Instrument Type Change Similarity for the Same Structure?

Earlier we found a difference between cross-library pairs recorded on matching versus different instrument types. But those two collections could contain **different molecules**. A direct comparison would be like comparing two witnesses who had been shown different suspects.

**A stricter control:** retain only `inchikey14` structures with both kinds of cross-library comparison in the research archive: matching and different instrument types. Compute the median cosine within each category. **Connect measurements of the same structure with a line.** The adduct is the same, and the difference in mean collision energy is at most 5 eV.

**Important distinction:** this controls for molecular connectivity; it is not an intervention that switches only the instrument while holding every other acquisition parameter fixed. Library protocols can differ in additional ways.
