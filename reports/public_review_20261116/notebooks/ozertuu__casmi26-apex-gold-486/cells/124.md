## 46 · Losing Cosine Similarity and Gaining New Peaks: One Story or Two?

We have so far studied the collision-energy experiment using two different measures. **Cosine similarity** describes how alike two spectra are. **The share of intensity in newly unmatched peaks** measures how much signal no longer aligns by `m/z`.

For **221 structures** with matched near-energy and distant-energy comparisons, we examine *two within-structure changes*: how much cosine decreases and how much the unmatched-peak intensity share rises. Agreement supports a changing fragmentation pattern rather than a single striking anecdote.

**Interpretation check:** both measures are derived from the same spectral peaks, so their correlation is not independent physical evidence.
