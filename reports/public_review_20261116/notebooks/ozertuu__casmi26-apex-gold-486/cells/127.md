## 📚 Scientific Sources and Reproducibility

**Dataset and task:** [Enveda CASMI 2026](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/enveda-CASMI26-molecule-id-mass-spectra/overview) — the public `train.parquet`, with known structures identified by `inchikey14`. Peaks, spectral libraries, and collision energies come from these measurements; they were **not predicted by a model**.

**Spectral comparisons:** cosine similarity with square-root intensity weighting and an explicit `m/z` tolerance. Its limitations are examined in the masking, ion-series, and control experiments above. Scientific context: [GNPS molecular networking](https://www.nature.com/articles/nbt.3597), [MassBank](https://massbank.eu/MassBank/), and [SIRIUS/CSI:FingerID](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26392543/).

**Part VIII data:** full metadata statistics across 21 Parquet row groups, followed by a purposefully sampled collection of 10,299 spectra. The 1,218 spectra truncated to their top 250 peaks were excluded from paired experiments. All percentages, distributions, and chemical examples in that part are calculated from the actual research archive. A compact numerical excerpt is embedded in the code so its plots can be reproduced without downloading the entire `train.parquet` again.

**What the evidence does and does not measure:** full-train statistics describe the dataset; the paired comparisons and 274 two-candidate duels diagnose mechanisms. They are not hidden-test submission scores.

**Dependence control:** Experiments 44–46 use saved pairs from the same diagnostic panel. In the uncertainty analysis for the duels, observations are clustered by molecular formula.
