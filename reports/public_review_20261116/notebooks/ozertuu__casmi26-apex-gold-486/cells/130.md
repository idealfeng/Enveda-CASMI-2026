## 00 · The Evidence Library: Data, Models and Reference Spectra

A molecular search engine needs more than the query spectrum. It requires **candidate structures**, trained spectrum and fingerprint models, structural priors and experimentally measured references. These resources play different roles: candidate databases decide what *can* be found, neural models evaluate how well a candidate explains a spectrum, and reference libraries supply independent measurements.

The public recipe uses the CASMI competition data, attributed V4G assets, PubChem and natural-product priors, fold-safe FPNet, the spectrum model, scorer v80 and two external MS/MS libraries. Exact resource links appear in **Sources and Authors** below. The inventory checks which evidence collections are available before candidate ranking begins.
