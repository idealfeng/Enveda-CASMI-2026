## 01 · A Ranking Recipe with Distinct Sources of Evidence

The public configuration combines a candidate consensus with a separately weighted top position and tail (`w_top=0.25`, `w_mid=0.75`). It uses the `MASS` correction for precursor compatibility, a fragmentation-based check and a measured-library match for exceptionally strong rank-1 evidence.

These are not interchangeable votes. An accurate mass is a constraint; predicted MS/MS similarity is a model estimate; a measured reference spectrum is direct experimental evidence. Keeping those roles distinct makes the final ranking easier to interpret.
