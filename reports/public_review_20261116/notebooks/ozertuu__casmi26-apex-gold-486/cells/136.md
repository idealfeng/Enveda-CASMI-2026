## 03 · The Foundation: Candidate Generation and Two Ranking Engines

The foundation is a public multi-engine pipeline. It searches a broad chemical candidate space, combines fingerprint-based evidence with learned ranking and compares simulated spectra from **ICEBERG** and **GLACIER** with observed MS/MS. A second engine extends the search through complementary candidate catalogs.

The goal is **coverage before precision**: a scorer cannot rank the correct molecule if candidate generation never finds it. The downstream stages refine the candidate list rather than replacing the search process.
