## 04 · The Evidence Flow: From Peaks to a Shortlist

Each spectrum yields several forms of evidence: precursor mass, adduct and polarity, observed fragments, model-predicted fragments, candidate frequencies and chemical plausibility. The workflow combines those signals in stages, retaining a recoverable baseline candidate order before later rescoring.

This is the bridge from our EDA to inference: the same sources of spectral ambiguity discovered in Part I now determine how the model distributes confidence among competing structures.
