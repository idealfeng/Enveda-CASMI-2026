## 04 · A Molecular Formula Is Not a Unique Address

**C₆H₁₂O₆** tells us how many atoms of each element are present, but not **how they are connected**. An accurate mass can therefore point to an entire family of different structures.

Count how many distinct `inchikey14` values share each formula, then examine their actual molecular drawings.
