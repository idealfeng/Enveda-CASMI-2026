## 05 · FragGap: Can the Molecular Graph Explain the Peaks?

Neural similarity can be strong without an obvious fragmentation pathway. **FragGap** provides a complementary structural check: fragment masses are approximated from molecular subgraphs formed by breaking at most two bonds, then compared with measured peaks using a narrow mass window.

The result is not a complete fragmentation mechanism. It is a fast chemical plausibility signal that can expose candidates with appealing model scores but weak fragment support.
