## 07 · Fragmentation Plausibility as an Independent Check

A high-scoring candidate should explain more than an abstract embedding similarity. The physical-rescoring component examines fragment support from the molecular graph and uses it to evaluate competing structures.

This type of evidence is especially relevant near the decision boundary: closely related candidates can share most peaks while differing in a small set of diagnostic fragments.
