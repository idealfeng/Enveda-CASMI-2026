## 09 · Mass Accuracy: A Constraint, Not a Complete Identity

A measured precursor places a powerful constraint on molecular composition. The `MASS` component penalizes candidates whose neutral masses are inconsistent with the inferred precursor and adduct. This helps exclude implausible structures before small spectral-score differences dominate the ranking.

But equal-formula isomers share exact mass. As Part I demonstrated, mass consistency narrows the search; **fragment-level evidence is what can separate structural alternatives**.
