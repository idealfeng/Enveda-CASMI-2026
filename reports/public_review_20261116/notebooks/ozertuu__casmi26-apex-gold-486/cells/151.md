## 10 · Consensus: When Several Rankers Disagree

No single model sees every aspect of the spectrum. The consensus combines a baseline candidate order, neural scores, fragmentation evidence and structural priors. Different weights for the top candidate and the remaining positions allow the system to protect a credible leader while still improving the rest of the list.

Identity-aware deduplication prevents alternate SMILES representations of the same molecule from occupying multiple candidate slots. The ranked list then moves closer to the final **top-25** answer.
