## 11 · Isomer Reranking: More Complexity Is Not Always More Information

Our isomer experiments exposed a recurring failure mode: near-identical formulas and many shared fragments can produce convincing but incorrect matches. A specialized isomer reranker is one possible response.

The selected public configuration instead uses the **mass-only** variant of this layer. It offers a useful principle for feature engineering: a model component should earn its place through measured improvement, not through its architectural complexity.
