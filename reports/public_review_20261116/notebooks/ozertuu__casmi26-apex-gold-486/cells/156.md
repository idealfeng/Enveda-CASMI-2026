## 12 · Measured Spectra: When a Reference Can Overrule the Ranking

A predicted spectrum is a learned hypothesis; a high-quality measured MS/MS reference is a second observation of the chemistry. The external-library overlay searches reference spectra with compatible precursor mass and ion mode, then evaluates peak-level similarity.

When the match clears strict requirements, its associated structure can be promoted to **rank 1**. The public implementation uses [Takumu Hata's extlib](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/takumuhata/casmi26-extlib) and [harmonized MassBank](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/samartalwar/casmi-2026-spectral-library-massbankharmonized). This is the clearest distinction between *predicted compatibility* and *observed spectral agreement*.
