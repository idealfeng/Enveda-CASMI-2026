## 14 · One Molecule, One Ranked List

The prediction is an ordered list of up to **25 distinct SMILES strings** per molecule. The final table uses the competition's `molecule_id,smiles` schema and preserves the official molecule order. A molecule with a strong candidate at rank 1 contributes more to MRR@25 than the same correct structure buried in the tail: ordering, not just candidate recall, is the ultimate objective.
