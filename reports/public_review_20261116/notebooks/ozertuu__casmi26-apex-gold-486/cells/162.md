## Credits & Attribution · The People and Work Behind the Pipeline

A strong spectrum-to-structure ranking system is a collective result. Our contribution is the **46-question spectral investigation in Part I**, the visual research narrative and a unified walkthrough connecting those findings to published inference methods. The candidate-retrieval engines, pretrained models, learned scorers, ranking strategies and experimental reference libraries in Part II are credited to their original authors.

### IV. Our Research Contribution

This publication places a **46-question deep EDA** before the inference pipeline. It examines acquisition frequency, formula ambiguity, isomer discrimination, collision-energy dependence, spectral matching artifacts, cross-library transfer and the structure of ranking errors. The experiments and retained figures build a scientific explanation for why each subsequent source of ranking evidence matters.

The model portion is a carefully attributed reproduction and guided reading of published work—not a claim to have invented the underlying engines, pretrained models or measured libraries.
