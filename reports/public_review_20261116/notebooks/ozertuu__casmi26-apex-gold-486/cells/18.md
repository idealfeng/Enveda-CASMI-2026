## 06 · How Many Structural Candidates Can One Formula Hide?

The same elemental formula does not imply a unique chemical structure. For each formula, count the competing `inchikey14` structures across the full training set. Then ask a question closer to candidate ranking: **how hard is the choice when a formula admits 2, 10, or 50 structures?**

This experiment measures the **remaining chemical search space**, even if the correct molecular formula is known perfectly.
