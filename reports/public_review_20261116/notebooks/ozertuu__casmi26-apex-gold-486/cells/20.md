Imagine photographing the same scene under different lighting. The subject stays the same, but some details disappear while others become visible. **Collision energy** can play a similar role in MS/MS by changing fragmentation pathways and observed peaks.

We examine three possibilities: how much the same structure can differ across spectra; whether different, near-mass structures can appear almost identical; and which similarities follow from adduct chemistry rather than a unique molecular structure.
