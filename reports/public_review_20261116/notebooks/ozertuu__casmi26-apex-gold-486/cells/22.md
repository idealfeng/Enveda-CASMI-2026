## How We Compare Spectra

For same-structure pairs, we match `inchikey14`, adduct, instrument, and library; for different-structure pairs, we also require similar `precursor_mz`. We compare normalized peaks within **±0.02 Da**.

First, retain a simple greedy cosine; then check whether swapping A↔B changes its result, and compare it with **symmetric matching**. For spectra containing more than 350 peaks, retain the 350 highest-intensity peaks.
