## 09 · Collision Energy: When Does a Molecule Stop Resembling Itself?

**Matched-pair comparison.** For each structure, calculate cosine for close collision energies (difference ≤ 5 eV) and distant energies (difference ≥ 20 eV), keeping instrument, adduct, and library matched.

Crucially, **each structure serves as its own control**; we are not comparing two unrelated populations of molecules.
