## 12 · Low-Mass Fragments: Does the Finding Depend on a 50% Cutoff?

A striking trend can arise from an arbitrarily chosen definition of “low mass.” Repeat the same paired experiment at four cutoffs: **30%, 40%, 50%, and 60% of precursor m/z**.

The measurement conditions and molecule-selection procedure remain unchanged. If the trend persists across cutoffs, it is harder to attribute it to one convenient threshold.
