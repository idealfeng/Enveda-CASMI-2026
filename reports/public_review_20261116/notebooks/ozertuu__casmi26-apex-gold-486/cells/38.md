## 13 · The Real Paradox: When Another Molecule Looks More Similar Than Your Own

For each structure, compare two similarities: its match to another spectrum of itself at a distant collision energy, and its match to a different structure with a nearby precursor mass.

Points above the diagonal are cases where **spectral similarity favors the wrong chemical connectivity**. This illustrates a limitation of straightforward spectral-library matching.
