## 14 · The Dimer Trap: Nearly Identical Spectra From Different Molecules

For the dimeric adduct `[2M+Na]+`, the monomer ion `[M+Na]+` can dominate a spectrum. Its mass largely reflects the molecule's mass and the adduct chemistry. Two structures with the same or very similar formulas may therefore exhibit strong shared peaks.

We examine **different structures** with nearby precursor m/z, matched instrument type, and matched adduct. These are deliberately challenging pairs, not a population-wide error rate.
