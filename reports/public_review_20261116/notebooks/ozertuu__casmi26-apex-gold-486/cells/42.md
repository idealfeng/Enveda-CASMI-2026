## 15 · How Much More Common Is Misleading Cosine Among Dimeric Adducts?

Compare dimeric and non-dimeric pairs **under the same selection rule** and with the same symmetric cosine algorithm. Report the share with `cosine ≥ 0.80`, group sizes, and 95% Wilson confidence intervals.

If dimers produce many near-perfect matches, the effect should appear across the selected near-mass pairs, not just in one striking example.
