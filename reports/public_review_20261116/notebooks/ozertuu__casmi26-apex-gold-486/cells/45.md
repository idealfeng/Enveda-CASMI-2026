## 16 · Is the Finding an Artifact of the ±0.02 Da Tolerance?

A peak-matching mass tolerance is a parameter of the comparison algorithm, not a property of the molecule. Recalculate **the same pairs of distinct structures** at tolerances from ±0.005 to ±0.10 Da. Analyze dimeric and other adducts separately.

If the effect exists only at one convenient threshold, it is not a robust property of the measured spectra.
