## 21 · Is It the Monomer Mask — or Simply Removing the Strongest Peaks?

Earlier, similarity dropped sharply after masking the expected monomer ion. But that operation may have removed nearly all useful spectral signal.

**Control:** for the very same spectra, remove the highest-intensity peaks instead of the calculated ion series, until a comparable fraction of spectral power remains. If both operations destroy the cosine score similarly, the drop cannot be attributed to dimer-ion chemistry alone.
