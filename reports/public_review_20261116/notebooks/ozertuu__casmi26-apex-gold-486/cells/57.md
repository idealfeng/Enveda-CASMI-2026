## 22 · Spectral Similarity: Matching m/z Values or Matching Intensities?

Keep the **same m/z positions** but repeatedly shuffle peak intensities within one member of each pair. This preserves the number of peaks and the possibility of mass matches, while breaking the relationship between peak position and intensity.

If the observed cosine is substantially higher than the shuffled control, the shared mass grid is not the whole story: the **relative intensity pattern** matters. This measures the information carried by spectral weights, not the accuracy of structure identification.
