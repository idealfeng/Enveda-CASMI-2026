## 26 · Neutral Losses: What Might the Molecule Have Lost?

The difference between precursor and fragment m/z can sometimes match the exact mass of a neutral molecule, such as **H₂O (18.0106 Da)** or **CO₂ (43.9898 Da)**. Such mass gaps suggest possible fragmentation pathways.
