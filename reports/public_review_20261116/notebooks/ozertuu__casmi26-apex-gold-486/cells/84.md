If some spectra of a molecule land in training and others in validation, we are testing **a new spectrum of an already-seen structure**. Grouping by `inchikey14` keeps that structure out of the training fold and tests **generalization to unseen structures**.

Both settings are useful, but they measure **different capabilities**. The appropriate split depends on how the downstream ranker will use reference spectra and candidate structures.
