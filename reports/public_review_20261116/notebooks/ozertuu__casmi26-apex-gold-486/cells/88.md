## 31 · Same Training Data, Very Different Validation Difficulty

For row-holdout fractions of 10%, 20%, 30%, and 50%, calculate the expected probability that a validation molecule is represented by at least one other spectrum in the training subset.

This is **the expected familiar-structure fraction under independent row splitting**. A genuine `inchikey14`-grouped split excludes any such overlap by construction. The question is not which split looks better, but **which real retrieval scenario each one represents**.
