## 32 · How Much of the Signal Is Spread Across Peaks?

**Question.** As collision energy increases, does the intensity distribution diversify, or do more weak peaks merely become detectable?

V12 traced the movement of intensity along `m/z`. Now we add **normalized spectral intensity entropy**. It approaches zero when one peak dominates and one when intensity is distributed more evenly. This is a descriptive summary of the distribution, **not a direct measure of chemical information**.

**Matched comparison.** Compare the lowest and highest collision energies **within the same `inchikey14`**, keeping the library, instrument, and adduct fixed. Require at least 10 eV separation and bootstrap the median difference **by structure**.

**Interpretation.** We can describe whether intensity distribution changes in matched pairs, not claim that one energy always yields better identification.
