## 33 · Can Peak Count Explain the Entropy Shift?

**Alternative explanation.** Perhaps the apparent entropy shift reflects the number of retained weak peaks rather than collision energy. The number of recorded peaks itself depends on spectral processing.

**Control.** Use **exactly the same structures and spectrum pairs** as Experiment 32, separating pairs according to the relative change in retained peak count. Compare the median entropy difference and its distribution across the two groups.

This tests the robustness of a descriptive observation; it is not a causal experiment, because acquisition and processing conditions may remain confounded.
