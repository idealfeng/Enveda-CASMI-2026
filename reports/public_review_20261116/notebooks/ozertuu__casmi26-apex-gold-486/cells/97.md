## 34 · One Structure in Two Libraries: A Fair Comparison?

V12 measured structural overlap between source libraries. But **matching `inchikey14` alone does not make spectra comparable**: adduct, instrument, and collision energy can all change the measurement.

Build a **comparability ladder** for cross-library pairs from the same structure in the diagnostic cohort:

**same structure → same adduct → same instrument → collision-energy difference ≤ 5 eV.**

Count both surviving pairs and unique structures. If enough matched observations remain, compare cross-library cosine against within-library cosine under similar restrictions. This is a diagnostic comparison, **not an isolated causal estimate of library effects**.
