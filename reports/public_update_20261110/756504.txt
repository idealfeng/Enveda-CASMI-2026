Hi all,

I've published my full pipeline for this competition: github.com/Majyles/kaggle-casmi-2026

It's far from the top of the leaderboard (0.313 public), but it may be useful as a clean, reproducible baseline, and for a few lessons on validation:


Validation that mimics the hidden test. Classes 1 and 2 are simulated by hiding reference spectra (by scorer key, across all libraries). The subset closest to the test is enveda-np-examples (timsTOF natural products).

Class 1 is cross-instrument. No public library contains timsTOF spectra of natural products, so a real class-1 match always compares timsTOF against other instruments, with a median entropy similarity of ~0.90 vs ~0.99 between public libraries. A ranker trained on naively simulated class 1 learns to distrust these matches. Simulating class 1 by hiding the whole instrument family, and dropping groups where the answer has no visible spectrum left, fixes most of it.

Learned ranker vs formula. A LightGBM lambdarank ranker beats the hand-tuned formula overall, mainly on class 2. Class weights act as a prior between "identical" and "analog" situations.


Everything is in a small Python package (casmi) with tests, pipeline scripts and research notebooks (notebook 05 walks through the ranker step by step).

Feedback welcome, and good luck to everyone for the final stretch!
